# Singapore Math placement tests (Primary Mathematics 1A-4B) - phrasing digest

Sources: free placement/assessment tests.
https://www.singaporemath.com/pages/placement-tests

## Structure worth borrowing
- **Number bonds** as a first-class representation from level 1A: "5, 3, and 2 make a number bond. Write the missing number for the number bonds." (part-part-whole thinking before equations)
- "Write 4 different addition or subtraction equations using the numbers 4, 3, and 7." (fact-family fluency as production, not recognition)
- Sub-parts (a)(b)(c)(d) share one stem - one context, several small asks. Cuts reading load per item.
- Marks in square brackets [2] per part.
- "Fill in the blanks. 4,900 is ___ more than 4,890." (comparison as a sentence to complete)

## Bar-model / heuristic phrasing (upper levels)
- "Mrs. Merry had 197 stickers. She gave 7 stickers to each of the students in her class. She had fewer than 7 stickers left over. How many students does she have?" - division-with-remainder as a real constraint, no keyword "divide".
- Word problems deliberately avoid operation keywords; the model must come from the situation.

## Extraction notes
- 438 items from 8 tests (1A-4B), fully parsed. Early-level items are picture-heavy (block counting, "Which set has more?") and are marked figure_dependent=true.
