# NY State released items (grades 3-4) - phrasing digest

Sources: nysedregents.org released questions, 2024, 2025, 2026.
https://www.nysedregents.org/ei/math/2026/2026-released-items-math-g3.pdf (and ...-g4.pdf; 2024/2025 same pattern)

## Structure worth borrowing
- Two item types: **multiple-choice** (4 options, distractors built from known misconceptions, e.g. "three thousand four hundred sixty" vs "three thousand four hundred six" for 3,406) and **constructed-response** (1-3 credits; 2+ credit items require "show your work").
- Real-world contexts with named children (Jaylani, Nellie): "Jaylani has 54 cookies. She puts all of the cookies into 9 bags. Each bag has the same number of cookies. How many cookies does Jaylani put into each bag?"
- Short declarative setup, one sentence per fact, question at the end. Numbers always as digits in text ("54 cookies"), never words, except when word-form itself is the skill.

## Patterns
- "Which expression represents how to find...?" (model selection over computation)
- "Which number sentence is true?" (evaluate all four)
- Pattern sequences: "2, 8, 14, 20, . . . What is the seventh number in the pattern?"
- Distractor design: options include the answer to the most common misreading (place-value slips, off-by-one in patterns, operation swaps).

## Extraction notes
- 2026 papers: near-complete (26/27 items per grade). 2024/2025 PDFs render some operands and question numbers as images; those items extract with gaps (marked number_gap=true) or are missing, so counts are partial (6-21 per paper). fi-ligature loss in these PDFs repaired heuristically.
