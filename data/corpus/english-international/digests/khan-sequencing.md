## Content
- `sequencing/khan-course-sequencing.json`: grade -> units -> skills. Grade 1: 3 units; grade 2: 8; grade 3: 14; grade 4: 14. 1,243 skill entries total.
- Unit titles alone are a useful cross-check against the Israeli curriculum map (e.g. Khan puts "Intro to multiplication" at the start of grade 3; money and time sit inside grade 2, not grade 1).
- Skill names are phrased as micro-competencies ("Numbers to 120", "Groups of ten objects", "2-digit place value challenge") - a good naming model for a skill graph.
