# White Rose Maths schemes of learning - phrasing and scaffolding digest

Sources: free schemes of learning, Years 1-4, all terms (12 PDFs).
https://assets.whiteroseeducation.com/new-schemes/Y{1-4}%20{Autumn,Spring,Summer}%20Term%20Scheme%20of%20Learning.pdf

## Structure worth borrowing
- Curriculum broken into **small steps** (a lesson-sized idea), each with: notes and guidance, **Key questions**, **Things to look out for** (misconceptions), **Possible sentence stems**, National Curriculum links. This is a complete pedagogical wrapper around every skill - and maps 1:1 onto what a tutor needs per skill: probing questions, expected misconceptions, language frames.
- CPA (Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract) stated explicitly; every step assumes manipulation before notation.

## Key questions (verbatim examples)
- "What is the same and what is different about...?" (their signature comparison prompt)
- "How do you know what order to write the digits when...?"
- "What patterns can you see in...?"
- "What does each letter/digit represent?"
- "Where have you seen this before?"

## Sentence stems (verbatim examples)
- "The letter ___ represents the number ___"
- "I know ___ is greater than ___ because ..."
- "There are ___ altogether."
- Stems carry a written blank (shown as a wide space in the PDFs; preserved as trailing space in extraction).

## Misconception framing ("Things to look out for")
- "Children may mix up which letter stands for which number."
- "Children may add the individual values instead of interpreting values by position."
- Directly usable as tutor error-diagnosis rules: each misconception maps to a wrong-answer pattern.

## Extraction notes
- 4,081 key questions and sentence stems extracted across Y1-Y4. Two-column PDF layout means some notes text leaked into early extraction; cleaned by filtering questions to "?" endings and stems to stem-like patterns. Blanks in stems lost their underscores in extraction (rendered as spacing in the PDFs).
