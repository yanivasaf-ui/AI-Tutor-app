# UK SATs (KS1 + KS2) - phrasing and scaffolding digest

Sources: gov.uk released test materials (2024, 2025). KS1 = Year 2 (grade 2 equivalent), KS2 = Year 6.
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/optional-key-stage-1-tests-2024-mathematics-test-materials
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/key-stage-2-tests-2024-mathematics-test-materials

## Structure worth borrowing
- Split of papers: **arithmetic** (pure computation, 1 mark, 25-36 items, tight time) vs **reasoning** (word problems, 1-3 marks, "Show your method" on 2-mark items). The split itself is a useful design: fluency and reasoning measured separately.
- Every item carries an explicit **mark value** shown to the child. 2-mark items always say "Show your method" - a correct method with a wrong answer still earns 1 mark. This teaches process-over-answer.
- KS1 reasoning starts with **aural questions read aloud by the teacher** ("Aural script: Look at the number line...") - scaffolds pre-fluent readers. Directly relevant for a voice tutor.

## Command verbs (verbatim, frequent)
- "Tick the two shapes that..." / "Tick all the calculations that..." (selection, not production)
- "Circle the two numbers that add together to make 70"
- "Write the missing number to make this multiplication correct."
- "Complete the number sentence below."
- "Match the name of each 3-D shape to its net."
- "Draw the reflection of the shaded shape about the mirror line."
- "How much change does Jack get? Show your method."
- "Explain how you know." / "Is he correct? Explain your answer."

## Scaffolding patterns
- Contexts are short (1-2 sentences) and concrete: shopping with pounds/pence, stickers in packs, juice in measuring jugs, class lunch times on clocks.
- Multi-step problems reveal the steps through layout (answer boxes per step), not through text instructions.
- Numbers chosen so the method matters more than the arithmetic (e.g. saving £2/week then £3/week toward £65).
- "Tick ALL that apply" forces evaluation of every option, unlike single-choice.

- 2024 KS1+KS2 papers fully parsed; 2025 KS1 fully, 2025 KS2 reasoning mostly. Arithmetic items are kept as equations. figure_dependent=true marks items whose stem references a figure the text extraction lost (clocks, shapes, measuring jugs).
